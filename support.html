Support

Swimo is a swim tracker for Apple Watch: pool laps, open-water distance, and the metrics you choose to see on your wrist.

Email support@swimo.me and include your Apple Watch model and a short description of what happened.

Subscriptions

Swimo Premium is billed by Apple. To change or cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. We never receive your payment details.

Health and location

Workout and heart-rate access is granted in the Health app. Location is used only during an open-water swim. You can change both any time in Settings.

Safety

Swimo is not a medical device. Distances and heart rate are estimates. Swim within your ability.
