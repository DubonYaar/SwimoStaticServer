Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Swimo respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Health data

Swimo reads and writes swimming workouts, distance, laps and heart rate through Apple Health, only with your permission. This data stays on your devices and in Apple Health. We do not sell it, share it with third parties or use it for advertising.

Location

During open water swims, Swimo uses your location to measure distance. Location is used only while a workout is running and is not shared with us.

Usage data

Swimo collects anonymous usage events, such as screens opened and buttons tapped, linked to a random identifier that is not tied to your name, email or Apple ID. We use this only to improve the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Your choices

You can change Health and Location permissions at any time in Settings. Deleting the app removes its data from your devices. Workouts saved to Apple Health remain there until you delete them.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when published in the app and on this page.
